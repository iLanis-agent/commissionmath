Commission Math

A tier rate applies to the slice, not the whole.

Boundary: arithmetic only Commission Math works out commission from the sales and rates you type. It does not know your plan's real terms, such as caps, accelerators, clawbacks or how sales are counted. Check those in your commission agreement.

The flat truth

Commission on 50,000.00 of sales at 5%: 2,500.00.

The tier truth

3% on the first 50,000.00 is 1,500.00; 5% on the other 10,000.00 is 500.00; total 2,000.00. The higher rate only touches the sales above the threshold.

The target truth

To earn 2,500.00 at 5%, sales of 50,000.00 are needed.
