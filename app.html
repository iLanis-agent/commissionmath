Commission Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out commission from the sales and rates you type. It does not know your plan's real terms, such as caps, accelerators, clawbacks or how sales are counted. Check those in your commission agreement.

The flat truth

One rate on all the sales.

The tier truth

One rate up to a threshold, another rate on the sales above it.

The target truth

The least sales that earn a target commission at one rate.

Commission Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
